BLOODLINK

Terms of Use

Last updated: November 2026


1. Educational purpose

BloodLink is developed for academic demonstration and learning. It is not a substitute for an authorised blood bank management system.


2. Synthetic data only

Users must enter fictional demonstration data only. Real donor, patient or medical records must not be entered.


3. Medical decisions

Blood testing, compatibility, approval, storage and transfusion decisions must be performed by qualified professionals under applicable regulations.


4. Blockchain limitations

Blockchain records can help demonstrate tamper-evident traceability. They do not prove that blood was correctly collected, tested, stored or transported.


5. No production use

This prototype has not been validated for real-world medical operations. Do not rely on it for actual blood inventory or emergency decisions.


6. Changes

These terms may be updated as the educational project develops.
